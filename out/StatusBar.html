Class: StatusBar

StatusBar(imgs, y, x, percentage)

Represents a status bar in the game (health, coins, bottles, or endboss). Handles the display and updating of the status bar based on game state.

Constructor

new StatusBar(imgs, y, x, percentage)

Creates a new StatusBar instance, loads the relevant images, and sets its position and value.

imgs string The type of status bar ("HEALTH", "COINS", "BOTTLE", "ENDBOSS").
