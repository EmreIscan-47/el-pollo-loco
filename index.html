Backstory...

In El Polo-Loco, you play as a daring adventurer in a sun-scorched desert, trying to save the crazy Chicken Kingdom. Mischievous chickens have scattered bottles and golden coins everywhere to keep intruders away. Collect all the coins to unlock the gate to the boss fight—a giant, wild chicken threatening to plunge the desert kingdom into chaos. Only the bravest can conquer the challenges and restore peace to the land!
